# Miriel

> Miriel is the context engine and platform for AI-native development: it gives AI apps and agents the context they need in real time, through a simple API, with every interaction encrypted and permissions managed at the token level.

Miriel is built by a team with backgrounds across Google, Tesla, Facebook, and Posit. The platform handles intelligent indexing and retrieval tailored to each data source, so developers can build personalized, auditable AI applications in minutes instead of months.
